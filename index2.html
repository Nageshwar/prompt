What is an AI Agent?
Moving from static chatbots to autonomous problem-solvers

💬 Standard Chatbot (Passive Responder)

  • Waiting: Sits passively waiting for a human to type into a prompt box.
  • Isolated: Has no connection to tools, calendars, email, or live college web pages.
  • Static Words: Generates helpful text, but cannot execute actual actions.
  • Analogy: Like asking an advisor a question in office hours—informative, but you still have to do all the manual work yourself.

🤖 Autonomous AI Agent (Perceive, Reason, Act)

  • Perceive: Continuously monitors web pages, webhooks, RSS feeds, or sensor data.
  • Reason: Evaluates conditions, parses structured schemas, and plans sequential steps.
  • Act: Triggers real-world tools (sends Telegram alerts, updates Google Calendar, writes Notion docs).
  • Analogy: Like an industrious student assistant working 24/7 in the background for your campus life.

Prompt Engineering Fundamentals
What is a Prompt?
The intern briefing mental model
The Core Insight: When using Google Search, you enter keywords (e.g., SSM college exam routine 2026). But LLMs don't index web pages—they predict contextually appropriate responses. Treat the AI as an eager intern who has read every textbook on Earth but has zero context on your specific situation.

❌ Vague Keyword Prompt

"Email professor extension CS102"

Why it fails: The AI has no idea who you are, why you need extra time, what assignment it is, or what tone your department expects.
Result: Generic fluff with [Insert Name] placeholders that looks sloppy.

✅ Structured Briefing Prompt

"Act as a polite university student. Write a 3-sentence extension request for CS-102 assignment to Prof. Ray due to illness. Ask for 24 hours."

Why it works: Supplies persona, context, precise constraints, and tone.
Result: A professional, ready-to-send draft that gets approved immediately.

Technique 3: Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting
Step-by-step deliberate reasoning for calculation accuracy
💡 Core Idea: Instruct the model to reason step-by-step before concluding, preventing math hallucinations.
🎯 Student Use Case: Fairly splitting shared student apartment rent and Wi-Fi based on actual days stayed.
📥 Complete Input Prompt
Calculate roommate Wi-Fi bill split step-by-step. Total bill is $60 for the month of January (30 days total). - Roommate Alex stayed all 30 days. - Roommate Sam went home for winter break and stayed only 10 days. - Roommate Chris stayed 20 days. Think step-by-step to calculate how much each person owes fairly based on person-days.
📤 Exact AI Model Output
Step 1: Calculate total person-days:
• Alex: 30 days | Sam: 10 days | Chris: 20 days
Total = 30 + 10 + 20 = 60 person-days.

Step 2: Calculate daily rate per person-day:
• $60 total bill / 60 person-days = $1.00 per person per day.

Step 3: Multiply each person's days by daily rate:
• Alex owes: 30 * $1.00 = $30.00
• Sam owes: 10 * $1.00 = $10.00
• Chris owes: 20 * $1.00 = $20.00

Final Balance: Alex: $30 | Sam: $10 | Chris: $20 (Total = $60.00).

Prompt Frameworks & Architecture
What is a Prompt Framework?
Structured cognitive scaffolding for reliable, repeatable AI outcomes
💡 Definition: A prompt framework is a standardized structural template or mental model that organizes instructions, background context, operational roles, and formatting constraints before querying an LLM. Instead of random conversational text, frameworks turn prompting into reliable, reproducible engineering.

🎯 Why Use Frameworks in Everyday Work?

  • 1. Eliminates Blank Slates: Provides ready-to-fill slots (Role, Task, Format) so you never wonder what to write.
  • 2. Prevents Drift & Hallucination: Constrains the LLM to precise boundaries, stopping unnecessary filler text.
  • 3. Enables Reliable Automation: Mandatory for n8n or Python pipelines where downstream tools require exact structures.
  • 4. Reproducible Across Teams: Allows student teams and labs to share, benchmark, and reuse prompt templates easily.

⚖️ Ad-Hoc Prompting vs. Framework-Driven

❌ Ad-Hoc (Unstructured):

"Write an email to my professor asking for more time on my assignment."
➔ Generates a 200-word template full of [Insert Name] placeholders and generic excuses.

✅ Framework-Driven (RTF Example):

[Role: Polite CS undergrad] +
[Task: Request 48h extension for CS-201 lab report due to viral fever] +
[Format: 3 sentences, course code, student ID, medical slip]
➔ Outputs a ready-to-send, approved professional email on the first try!
